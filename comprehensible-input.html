1. Preview 2–3 useful words (2 min). 2. Listen or watch for the main message (5–8 min). 3. Rewatch one unclear part or check the Japanese transcript (2–3 min). 4. Answer the one question, then move on (1 min).

Grammar video ≠ comprehensible listening. Shiro Neko explains the daily topic; the Nihongo no Jikan video supplies slow Japanese with visuals and a transcript.
